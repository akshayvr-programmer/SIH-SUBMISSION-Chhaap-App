## What the video shows

1. The problem: marginalized artisans locked out of online marketplaces by literacy, language, and workflow barriers.
2. The proposed solution: a voice-first, photo-to-listing flow in the artisan's own language.
3. Main workflow: photo capture and cleanup, craft recognition with human confirmation, voice-to-listing, fair pricing, and one-tap multi-channel publish (ONDC, GeM, WhatsApp).
4. The working prototype end-to-end, from a single photo and spoken sentence to a published listing.
