## One-paragraph summary

Chhaap lets a marginalized artisan list, price, and sell handcraft using only their voice and their own language. One photo and a spoken description become a clean catalogue image, a polished listing in the artisan's language, a fair market-aware price with a transparent floor, and a one-tap publish to ONDC, GeM, and WhatsApp. The interface speaks at every step and works across 12 Indian languages, so it reaches artisans who cannot read or type in English. The AI proposes; the artisan always decides.
